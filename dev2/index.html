    👋 Welcome!

    A few quick tips to get started
    • 🎓 Study Signals — tap a category to expand it and see every signal in it.
    • 🏈 Quiz — pick the right meaning, several rounds of 6, and build your streak!
    • ⏱️ Timed Quiz — race the clock through every signal in the book; a wrong answer just sends that one to the back of the line, so keep going until you've cleared them all!
    • 📋 Play Calls — look up any play's diagram and full signal sequence. Flip the Wing/Motion/Direction/Boot toggles (or, for Split formation plays, Split L/R and Pass) to see exactly how the call and the diagram change. Tap any player's number to give him an orange glow so it's easy to track just his job — set your position from the name menu and this happens automatically every time you open a play.
    • 🧠 Play Quiz — watch a play's signals, then set the matching Wing/Motion/Play/In-Out/Direction/Boot answer. Starts easy and gets tougher round by round.
    • 👤 Your name in the top-right corner is you — tap it for My Stats (your best scores and where you rank) or to sign out and switch players.
    • 🏆 Tap the trophy anytime for the leaderboard — Overall, Timed, Quiz, and Play Quiz tabs, all shared across the whole team. Overall tallies points from where you place on the Quiz, Timed, and Play Quiz boards.
    • 🌙 Tap the moon/sun to switch between dark and light mode.
    • 🔊 Tap the speaker to mute or unmute the music.

    🏈 2 Minute Drill

    🏈

    Ready to run it back?

    Read the signal. Beat the clock. Drive for the score.

    You're shown a coach's hand signals for a play call. Pick the right call from 4 close options before the clock runs out. Get it right and watch the play develop -- get it wrong and it's a false start, 5 yards back. String correct calls together for bonus yardage, break one outside for extra yards and a clock stoppage, and drive 75 yards for a touchdown. New drive starts if you've still got time left.
