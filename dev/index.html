📋 Play Calls — What's New

This tab is a coach-only preview. It's not part of the live study experience for players yet — think of it as a work-in-progress you're checking before it goes out.

Each play has two independent toggles right on the card: Wing L/R (which side #4 lines up on) and Direction L/R (which way the play actually goes). They don't have to match — flip either one any time.

▶ Play animates the diagram. Tap any player's circle to highlight just his path. The small 1x / ½x toggle slows the play down.

The flip icon (bottom-right) turns the card over to show the actual signal call for whatever Wing/Direction combo is currently selected — one sign at a time, looping twice.

How updates get made: route and blocking edits happen in the separate builder tool. Once you're happy with a change, it gets folded back into this file. Nothing goes live to players until you personally push the updated file to GitHub — that stays entirely your call.
