Frequently Asked Questions

How are the teams structured?▾
Single (e.g. 10, 11, 12, 13) or combined ages if necessary (e.g. 9/10, 12/13), determined in July based on registrations. Teams compete against other Central Mass towns offering the same program.
What gear will my athlete need?▾
Mouth guard, helmet, shoulder pads, black integrated pants, and football cleats. Certified helmet and shoulder pads are provided by the association. Players supply their own mouth guard, integrated pants, water bottle, and cleats.
What's included with registration?▾
Pre-season Jamboree Game, 8 regular season games, playoffs (record dependent), and equipment: helmet, shoulder pads, reversible jersey.
What are the parental commitments?▾
Fundraising: sell 10 raffle tickets ($10 each) by September 15.
Volunteering: as needed throughout the season (chains, MPRs, announcing, events).
Equipment: in-season care/cleaning, prompt return post-season.
Paperwork: player packet, original birth certificate (if new), report card, and a current physical -- all required to receive equipment.
When and where are practices?▾
90-120 minutes, three times per week. Days/times set by coaching staff, announced before the season. Held at Lunenburg High School fields or Taylor Field in Shirley. Additional practices possible around weather or playoffs, at the coach's discretion.
What can I expect on game day?▾
Season runs late July through October. Games run ~120 minutes. 4 home games, played at Lunenburg or Ayer-Shirley High Schools. Away games follow the CMYAF schedule across Central Mass. Players arrive 60 minutes before kickoff.
How do I stay informed?▾
Email and Facebook. Team Moms communicate your team's schedule and changes -- keep your SportsEngine email current. Join the Bengals Facebook Group and your team's group if one exists.
