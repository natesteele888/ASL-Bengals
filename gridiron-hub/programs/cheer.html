Frequently Asked Questions

What gear will my athlete need?▾
Cheer sneakers, socks, hair bow, and warm-up suit -- provided by the family. Warm-ups are ordered by the Bengals; hair bows are ordered by the coach.
What's included with registration?▾
Pre-season Jamboree (sideline), 8 regular season games (sideline), football playoffs if the team qualifies (sideline), 2-5 competitions depending on placement, and equipment: uniform and pompoms.
What are the parental commitments?▾
Fundraising: sell 10 raffle tickets ($10 each) by September 15.
Volunteering: as needed throughout the season (game day needs, events).
Equipment: in-season care/cleaning, prompt return post-season.
Paperwork: cheerleader packet, payment, original birth certificate, report card, and a current physical -- required to receive equipment.
When and where are practices?▾
90-120 minutes, twice per week. Days/times set by coaching staff, announced before the season. Held at Taylor Field in Shirley, or the Lunenburg High School / Ayer-Shirley Middle School gyms.
What can I expect on game day?▾
Season runs late July through October. Squads cheer sidelines for games 90 (flex) to 120 (tackle) minutes long. 4 home games, at Lunenburg (2) and Ayer-Shirley (2) High Schools. Cheerleaders arrive 30 minutes before kickoff for warm-ups.
What can I expect at competitions?▾
All Bengals squads are competitive and compete in at least 2 (fun & local), with a chance at up to 5 (states, regionals, nationals) depending on how far they advance -- see the 2026 schedule above.
How do I stay informed?▾
Email and Facebook. Coaches communicate your team's schedule and changes -- keep your SportsEngine email current. Join the Bengals Facebook Group and your team's group if one exists.
