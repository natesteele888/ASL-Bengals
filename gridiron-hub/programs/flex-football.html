FOOTBALL & CHEER

Flex Football

Designed by NFL players to replicate their practice regimen -- limited-contact football that teaches technique and game understanding, and the natural progression toward tackle.

Why Flex

Played 7-on-7 (ages 5-6) or 9-on-9 (ages 7-8), Flex is similar to flag football in that tackling is prohibited -- players learn concepts, skills, and positions in a safe environment, with more focus on the running/passing game, blocking, and pass rush than flag alone offers.

With nearly 50 Flex players in 2024, ASL Bengals was one of the few Central Mass programs to field two teams per division (6U & 8U).

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the teams structured?▾
Two teams: 6U (ages 5-6) and 8U (ages 7-8). 6U plays 7-on-7, 8U plays 9-on-9, on a 40-yard field. Teams compete against other Central Mass towns offering the same program.
What gear will my athlete need?▾
Light shoulder pads, soft-shelled helmet, flags, and a game jersey -- all provided by the Bengals. Players supply their own mouth guard, black shirt/shorts, water bottle, and footwear (cleats recommended).
What's included with registration?▾
Pre-season Jamboree Game, 8 regular season games, the Mighty Might Bowl, and equipment: helmet, shoulder pads, jersey, flags.
What are the parental commitments?▾
Fundraising: sell 10 raffle tickets ($10 each) by September 15.
Volunteering: as needed throughout the season.
Equipment: in-season care/cleaning, prompt return post-season.
Paperwork: player packet, original birth certificate (if new), and a current physical -- required to receive equipment.
When and where are practices?▾
90 minutes, twice per week. Days set by the coach, announced before the season. Held at Lunenburg High School fields or Taylor Field in Shirley.
What can I expect on game day?▾
Season runs late July through October. Games run ~90 minutes. 4 home games at Lunenburg or Ayer-Shirley High Schools. Away games follow the CMYAF schedule. Players arrive 30 minutes before kickoff.
How do I stay informed?▾
Email and Facebook. Team Moms communicate your team's schedule and changes -- keep your SportsEngine email current. Join the Bengals Facebook Group and your team's group if one exists.
