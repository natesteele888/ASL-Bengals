Registration FAQ

Do I need to pay in full at registration?

No -- a minimum 50% deposit is due at registration. The remaining balance is charged to the card on file closer to the season (confirm the exact date with the Bengals -- the source page listed a date that appears to be left over from a prior season).

What are my payment options?

A minimum 50% deposit via debit or credit card. Note: SportsEngine adds credit card processing fees at checkout.

What if I'm eligible for the family maximum?

There's a per-family maximum of $500. Email aslbengals.president@gmail.com for a discount code before registering.

What about scholarships?

A limited number of scholarships are available each season for families having difficulty with registration fees. Email aslbengals.president@gmail.com for more information.

When do I get the birth certificate back?

Original birth certificates are required for new athletes only. Returning players use a certified copy already on file. Team Moms return original certificates once player books are certified by CMYFCC in August.

What if I need to cancel?

Once registered and paid, athletes are not eligible for a refund after Jamboree Weekend, regardless of reason -- personal, medical, or otherwise. Registration insurance (if purchased at checkout) is a separate SportsEngine product; contact them directly for that.
