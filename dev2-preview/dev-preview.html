Routes & blocks — this lineup only
Drag the orange handle to where this player's block or route should actually finish. Only the players this lineup moved get one — everyone else still has the assignment the play was drawn with.

Mirror saves a starting point for the other side (Left/Right) from what's here now, then switches you to it so you can check it with Play and adjust anything that doesn't look right — it isn't kept in sync afterward, the two sides are independent from that point on.
Ball path — this play, every formation and side
Tapping just one player says he carries it the whole play -- that's enough to fix "this play hands off to the wrong back," no second tap needed. The exchange point places itself between the two players when you tap a second — drag a numbered badge only if it's in the wrong spot. Set the word on each leg: a pitch and a pass are taught differently, and on a pop pass the pass leg is where it gets released.

A flagged leg means a route or formation change moved that receiver away from where this exchange was set — tap Snap to route on it to put the exchange back on him.

This sequence belongs to the play, not this lineup — Left and Right, and every formation this play can be called from, all use it. There's no way yet to give one formation its own different ball carrier for the same play.
